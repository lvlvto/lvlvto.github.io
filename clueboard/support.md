**Clueboard Noir** — murder-mystery logic puzzles. Place the suspects, find the killer.

Found a case that seems impossible, or a statement you think is wrong? Email
**kursoftware@gmail.com** with the case number (or "daily case" and the date), your
device model and iOS or Android version.

## Frequently asked

### How do I play?

Every row and every column of the floor plan holds exactly one person. Nobody stands
on a table, plant, shelf or TV — chairs, beds and carpets are fine. Every statement is
true, and the murderer is the only person who was in the same room as the victim. Tap
a person, then a square to place them; tap an empty square to cross it out. The rules
are always one tap away under the **?** button on a case.

### Does every case have exactly one solution?

Yes. Every case is checked by a solver before you see it, so the statements always lead
to one arrangement and one killer — no guessing needed.

### A statement turned red. What does that mean?

The people it mentions are placed somewhere that makes it false. Move one of them and
the statement turns back to normal.

### How do hints work?

A hint places one person in their correct square and pins them there. You start with
three. When they run out, you can watch a short video for one more. Clueboard Noir Pro gives
unlimited hints.

### How do I remove the ads?

Buy **Clueboard Noir Pro** from the ⋮ menu on the home screen → Remove ads. It is a one-time
purchase that removes every ad and unlocks unlimited hints.

### I reinstalled the app and Pro is gone.

Open the ⋮ menu on the home screen and tap **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same
Google or Apple account you bought it with.

### Will my progress move to a new phone?

No. Solved cases, best times and hints are saved only on the device, and are removed if
you uninstall the app. Pro can always be restored, progress cannot.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes. Cases are generated on the device, so you can play in airplane mode. Ads and the
hint video need a connection.

### How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, open the ⋮ menu on the home screen →
**Privacy options**.
