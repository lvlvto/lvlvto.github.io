**Clueboard Treasure** — treasure-hunt logic puzzles. Read the map scraps, dig up the
treasure.

Found a map that seems impossible, or a scrap you think is wrong? Email
**kursoftware@gmail.com** with the map or trail number (or "daily map" and the date),
your device model and iOS or Android version.

## Frequently asked

### How do I play a map?

Each map hides a chain of finds, and the last one is the treasure. Read the scraps
under the map, rule squares out by tapping or dragging across them, then switch to
**Dig**, tap the square you believe in and confirm. Every find hands you the scraps for
the next one. Nothing is buried under a landmark, and no two finds share a row or a
column. The rules are always one tap away under the **?** button.

### What do "steps", "north of" and "the shore" mean?

A step is one square up, down, left or right, never diagonal. "North of the Palm" means
any row higher up the map, not only the squares straight above it; "west of" means any
column further left. The shore is the outer ring of squares.

### What is a forged scrap?

From map 51 on, some finds come with a forgery: exactly one of their scraps is false.
The find lies on the one square that breaks exactly one scrap. A square that fits every
scrap is bait, and digging there springs a trap that costs two shovels. Tap a square
once to note that it breaks one scrap, twice to rule it out.

### What happens when I dig in the wrong place?

A wrong dig breaks a shovel, and you have three per map. When all are broken you can
start the map again, or watch a short video for one more shovel.

### How do trails work?

The steps of a route got shuffled. Drag them into the order that walks from the flag
without leaving the island, running into a landmark or crossing its own tracks. Only
one order works. When the red line runs all the way, dig at the ✕.

### Does every puzzle have exactly one solution?

Yes. Every map and every trail is checked before you see it: the scraps always pin each
find to one square, and exactly one order of the steps can be walked. No guessing is
needed.

### How do hints work?

A small hint is free and points at the scrap worth reading next. A full hint does the
reasoning for you: it rules squares out, exposes a forged scrap, or shows the last
square left. On a trail it puts the next step in its place. You start with three full
hints; when they run out, you can watch a short video for one more. Using hints or
breaking shovels lowers the stars you earn for a map.

### Can I remove the ads?

Not at the moment. The app is free and has no purchases. The video ads are always
optional; the banner and the occasional ad between puzzles are what keeps it free.

### Will my progress move to a new phone?

No. Solved maps and trails, stars, best times and hints are saved only on the device,
and are removed if you uninstall the app.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes. Maps and trails are generated on the device, so you can play in airplane mode. Ads
and the videos for hints or shovels need a connection.

### How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, open the ⋮ menu on the home screen →
**Privacy options**.
