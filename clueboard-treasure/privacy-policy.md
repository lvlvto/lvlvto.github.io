## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer ("Developer", "we", "us") collects,
uses, and protects information when you use the Clueboard Treasure mobile application
(the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance
with this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

The App has no user accounts and does not collect personally identifiable information
such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Your progress (solved maps and trails, stars, best times, remaining hints, the daily
map) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us and is deleted when you
uninstall the App.

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data about your
device and how you use the App:

- **Google AdMob** — serves the advertisements shown inside the App.
- **Google Firebase Analytics** — anonymous usage data, such as which maps and trails
  are started and solved, how long they take, how many hints are used and how often a
  dig misses.

The App offers no purchases and never asks for payment details.

The data collected through these services may include:

- app usage statistics and interaction data,
- device information (e.g. device model, operating system version, language),
- advertising identifiers — the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and, on iOS, the
  Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — as well as other anonymized identifiers,
- coarse, IP-derived location (country/region level) used to serve relevant ads.

## How We Use the Information

Collected data is used to:

- display and measure advertisements,
- understand which maps and trails are too hard or too easy and improve the puzzles,
- analyze app performance and fix problems.

## Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the
home screen and the list of maps and trails, an occasional full-screen ad between
puzzles, and an optional video you can choose to watch for an extra hint or for one
more shovel after a failed hunt.

- **In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, the App asks for
  your consent when it starts, using Google's certified consent management platform
  (User Messaging Platform). You can review or change your choice at any time from the
  **⋮ menu on the home screen → Privacy options**.
- **On iOS**, the IDFA is only available to advertisers if you allow tracking in Apple's
  App Tracking Transparency permission. You can change this at any time in
  **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- If you decline consent or tracking, the App continues to work and shows
  **non-personalized ads** instead.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising system-wide: on Android via
  **Settings → Google → Ads**, on iOS via the Tracking setting above.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to third parties.

Data is shared with the service provider listed above (Google LLC, for AdMob and
Firebase) solely so it can provide those services. Advertising identifiers are shared
with Google and its advertising partners for the purposes of serving and measuring ads,
which may constitute "tracking" under applicable rules. Data may be processed and stored
on servers located outside your country of residence.

Third-party privacy policy:

- Google (AdMob, Firebase): <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect collected data. However, no method of electronic
transmission or storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal
data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with such data, please
contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data
relating to you, and to withdraw consent for personalized advertising. Requests can be
sent to the contact address below. Withdrawing advertising consent is also possible
directly in the App from the **⋮ menu on the home screen → Privacy options**.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective
immediately upon posting the updated policy.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Developer by
email at kursoftware@gmail.com or using the contact information provided in the Google
Play store or App Store listing.
