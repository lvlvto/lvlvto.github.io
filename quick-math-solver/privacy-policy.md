## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer ("Developer", "we", "us") collects,
uses, and protects information when you use this mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance
with this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name,
email address, phone number, or precise location.

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data about your
device and how you use the App:

- **Google AdMob** — serves the advertisements shown inside the App.
- **Google Firebase Analytics** — anonymous usage and diagnostic data.
- **RevenueCat** — processes the ad-free (Pro) purchase.

The data collected through these services may include:

- app usage statistics and interaction data (e.g. which features are used),
- device information (e.g. device model, operating system version, language),
- advertising identifiers — the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and, on iOS, the
  Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — as well as other anonymized identifiers,
- coarse, IP-derived location (country/region level) used to serve relevant ads,
- crash reports and performance data,
- purchase and subscription status (no payment card data ever reaches us).

## How We Use the Information

Collected data is used to:

- display and measure advertisements, including limiting how often the same ad
  is shown to you,
- analyze app performance and identify and fix bugs and crashes,
- improve functionality and user experience,
- monitor overall app usage trends,
- unlock and restore the ad-free (Pro) purchase.

## Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. Google and
its ad partners may use advertising identifiers and similar technologies to serve
personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure ad performance.

- **In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, we ask for
  your consent before any personalized advertising takes place, using Google's
  certified consent management platform (User Messaging Platform). You can review or
  change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options** inside the App.
- **On iOS**, the App asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency
  prompt before the IDFA is used for tracking. You can change this at any time in
  **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- If you decline consent or tracking, the App continues to work and shows
  **non-personalized ads** instead.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising system-wide: on Android via
  **Settings → Google → Ads**, on iOS via the Tracking setting above.
- Purchasing the ad-free (Pro) upgrade disables all advertising in the App.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to third parties.

purposes of serving and measuring ads, which may constitute "tracking" under
applicable rules. Data may be processed and stored on servers located outside your
country of residence.

Third-party privacy policies:

- RevenueCat: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with such data,
please contact us and we will delete it.
