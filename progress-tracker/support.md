**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

**Progo** — goals, projects and streaks, with a Home Screen widget.

Something not working, or a feature you're missing? Email
**kursoftware@gmail.com** and include your device model and iOS or Android version.
A screenshot helps a lot if something looks wrong.

## Frequently asked

### How do I add the widget?

First open the activity in Progo and turn on **Pin to Widget**. Only pinned
activities can be shown on a widget.

- **iPhone:** touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap **Edit** →
  **Add Widget**, find Progo and pick the small or medium size. Then touch and
  hold the widget, choose **Edit Widget** and select the activity.
- **Android:** touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap **Widgets**,
  find Progo and drag it onto the screen. Pick the activity on the screen that
  opens next.

### Can I update progress from the widget?

Yes. Tap **+1** on a goal, or tick off a step of a project, without opening the
app.

### My widget shows old numbers

Open Progo once — the widgets refresh when the app syncs. If the activity is
gone from the list, check that **Pin to Widget** is still on.

### Do I need an account?

No. Progo works without signing up, and your activities are stored on your
phone. It also works offline. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, never
the content of your activities — details in the
[Privacy Policy](/progress-tracker/privacy-policy).

### What happens to my data if I delete the app?

It's deleted with the app. Your activities live only on your device, so
removing Progo removes them too. You can also wipe everything yourself in
Settings → **Clear All Data**.

### How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Open Settings → **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google
account you bought it with.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by the app. On iPhone go to
Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android open Google Play → profile
icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

### Why didn't I get a reminder?

Check that notifications for Progo are allowed in your phone's settings. On
Android, battery saving can also delay reminders.

### Which languages are supported?

English, Polish, German and Spanish. Change it in Settings → Language.
