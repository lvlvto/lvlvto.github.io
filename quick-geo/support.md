**QuickGeo: Geography Quiz** — flags, capitals and US states.

Something not working, or an answer you think is wrong? Email
**kurmerch@gmail.com** and include your device model and iOS or Android version.
Reports about a specific country or state are especially welcome — please say
which mode you were playing.

## Frequently asked

### How do I change how long I get per question?

Settings → Time per question. There are three presets — 5, 10 and 15 seconds —
plus a custom value you can type in.

### Can I answer by swiping instead of tapping?

Settings → Answer input → Swipe. It applies to Flags, Capitals, the flashcards
and the daily question. The four-answer quizzes are always tapped.

### Which countries and states show up?

You pick the regions — Europe, Americas, Asia, Africa, Oceania and US States —
on the sheet that opens before a Flags, Capitals or Flashcards round. That choice
is remembered, and the Flag Quiz and Capital Quiz use it too, so change it there
if a four-answer quiz is asking about the wrong part of the world.

### What is Weak spots?

It builds a round out of the countries you get wrong most often, worked out from
your own answer history. Play a few rounds first — it needs something to go on.

### What is the daily challenge?

One question per day, plus a calendar of the current month. A green tick is a day
you got right, an amber cross a day you missed, and you can still go back and
answer a day you skipped.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every question, flag and map is bundled in the app, so a round plays fine in
airplane mode.

### Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. QuickGeo is paid once, and everything in it is unlocked from the first
launch. No ads, no subscriptions, no extra content to buy.

### How do I clear my statistics?

Open Stats and use the clear button there. It wipes your answer history, which
also empties Weak spots.
